Projects

A selection of my research, academic and community projects. Research interests: Explainable AI, Machine Learning, Data Mining and Cloud Computing.

Research Projects

  • An Explainable Deep Learning Approach for Automated Plant Disease Identification and Classification (MSc research, University of Moratuwa). Combining CNN models with explanation methods such as Grad-CAM, LIME and SHAP so that plant disease predictions can be understood and trusted. Planned datasets: PlantVillage and PlantDoc. Status: in progress.
  • Explainable AI for Cybersecurity Incident Response and Cloud Security. Ongoing research on applying explainable AI to help security analysts understand and act on automated detections in cloud environments. Status: ongoing.
  • LLM Adoption in Sri Lankan Higher Education. An independent research project studying how large language models are adopted by students and lecturers in Sri Lankan universities. Status: in progress.

Academic and Software Projects

  • Queue Using Two Stacks (MSc, Programming and Program Design). Implementation and testing of a queue built from two stacks, with a written report and a recorded video demonstration.
  • Hospital Management System (sample project proposal). A complete example proposal covering the SDLC flow, three-tier architecture, use case and ER diagrams, a Gantt chart and UI wireframes, prepared for Higher Diploma students.
  • Library Management System (Python). A teaching scenario and practical assignment built for the AI and Machine Learning module.

Community and Leadership Projects

  • Global Entrepreneurship Week (GEW) 2026, ICST University Park. A month-long programme of workshops, guest lectures, school engagements and regional outreach, coordinated by the Industrial Linkage Unit.
