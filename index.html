Hi, I am Mohamed Aasik

I am Mohamed Aasik, an IT Lecturer at the Faculty of Computing, International Campus of Science and Technology (ICST), with a strong interest in teaching, research, technology, and professional development.


I am a dedicated academic professional with experience in higher education, curriculum development, student mentoring, research, and industry engagement. My teaching and research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Mining, Explainable AI (XAI) and Cloud Computing, with a particular interest in applying intelligent and explainable technologies to cloud security, incident response, and real-world computing challenges.


Alongside my academic career, I have gained industry experience in the IT sector, which has provided me with practical exposure to software development, cloud technologies, virtualization, and modern IT infrastructure. This industry experience enables me to connect theoretical concepts with practical applications and bring industry-oriented perspectives into my teaching and student projects.


I am also actively involved in student leadership, volunteering, and technology-focused initiatives. I have contributed to organizing workshops, hackathons, industry engagement activities, professional development programmes, and technology events, while working with students and external industry and professional organizations. These experiences have strengthened my skills in event management, teamwork, leadership, communication, industry collaboration, and community engagement.


I am passionate about continuous learning, knowledge sharing, research, and empowering students to develop the technical and professional skills required to succeed in the rapidly evolving technology industry. My long-term goal is to contribute to meaningful research and innovative technology solutions while continuing to grow as an educator, researcher, and IT professional.
